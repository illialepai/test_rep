Username styles

Statuses are an estimate from each character's Unicode properties against TikTok's rule (letters, numbers, _ and periods). TikTok decides when you save, so no style is guaranteed.

Apply it to TikTok

  1. Copy a generated username. Click it or press Copy.
  2. Open the TikTok app.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Tap Edit profile.
  5. Tap Username.
  6. Clear the field and paste the generated username.
  7. Tap Save. If TikTok rejects it, try a style marked Likely compatible.

TikTok lets you change your username once every 30 days. This tool never asks for or stores your TikTok login, and it doesn't connect to TikTok. Everything runs in your browser.

Not affiliated with TikTok. Unicode styles may display differently on some devices.
